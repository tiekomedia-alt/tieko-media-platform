## 2. Global writing rules

All public copy must:

- Use British English
- Avoid em dashes
- Use specific, direct language
- Lead with the user's problem or desired outcome
- Describe Tieko as a digital intelligence and infrastructure company
- Avoid generic agency slogans
- Avoid unsupported superlatives
- Avoid guaranteed results
- Avoid manufactured urgency
- Avoid inflated technology language
- Avoid keyword stuffing
- Identify regulated or partner-delivered services accurately
- Use Book a Call as the primary CTA
- Answer important questions before requesting conversion
- Remain useful when a visitor enters directly from search or an advertisement

## 3. Global CTA hierarchy

## 4. Global proof rules

Proof may include:

- Verified case studies
- Named products such as TeamShuffle
- Approved client work
- Verifiable campaign outcomes
- Published research
- Defined methodology
- Professional credentials
- A verified Google rating with current review count
- Original tools such as the Digital Business Health Check

Do not use:

- Invented testimonials
- Unverified logos
- Unsourced statistics
- Fake dashboards
- Anonymous claims presented as client evidence
- Placeholder awards
- Fabricated project metrics
