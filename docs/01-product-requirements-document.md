## 1. Document purpose

This Product Requirements Document defines what the Tieko Media platform must do, who it must serve, which capabilities are required for launch and how completion will be evaluated.

It converts the Master Project Brief into functional, content, conversion, administrative, security, SEO, accessibility and performance requirements. It intentionally avoids prescribing final implementation details that belong in the Technical Design Document.

## 2. Product summary

The Tieko Media platform will be a multi-page, search-led business website and publishing platform for an African digital intelligence and infrastructure company.

The platform must:

- Explain Tieko Media’s positioning clearly
- Present eight priority services
- Generate qualified booked calls and enquiries
- Present original digital products
- Support paid intelligence-report commissioning
- Publish insights, reports and case studies
- Establish authority through useful, evidence-led content
- Support organic discovery and page-specific advertising
- Collect data securely
- Scale to additional services, products and content without architectural rework

The platform is not a static digital brochure. It is a business-development, publishing, authority-building and conversion system.

## 4. Product principles

### 4.1 Build from user intent

Every page must answer a real visitor question, support a defined journey or satisfy a distinct search intent.

### 4.2 One platform, multiple entry points

A visitor may enter through the homepage, a service page, a report, an article, a case study or a product page. Each important page must explain enough context to function as an independent landing page.

### 4.3 Proof before promotion

Claims must be supported through work, products, case studies, reports, client evidence or transparent methodology.

### 4.4 Clarity over service sprawl

The platform may contain many capabilities, but they must be organised into understandable categories rather than presented as an undifferentiated list.

### 4.5 African context, global quality

The platform must feel culturally grounded, commercially credible and technically competitive internationally.

### 4.6 Security and privacy by design

Data collection, storage and access must be limited, justified and protected from the beginning.

### 4.7 Performance is part of the design

Visual ambition must not compromise loading speed, accessibility or usability.

### 4.8 Content must be maintainable

Authorised non-developers must be able to manage approved editorial content without changing application code.

## 8. Core user journeys

### 8.1 Service discovery to booked call

1. Visitor enters a service page.
2. Visitor understands the problem and offering.
3. Visitor reviews the process, deliverables, proof and FAQs.
4. Visitor selects Book a Call.
5. Visitor reaches the booking experience with service context preserved.
6. Visitor completes a booking through the approved scheduling system.
7. Relevant conversion events are recorded.

### 8.2 Paid campaign landing-page journey

1. Visitor arrives through a tracked advertisement.
2. Landing page matches the advertisement’s promise.
3. Visitor sees a focused offer with minimal distraction.
4. Visitor reviews proof, deliverables and eligibility.
5. Visitor books a call or submits a relevant enquiry.
6. Campaign, source and landing-page attribution are preserved.

### 8.3 Intelligence-report commissioning journey

1. Visitor enters through the Intelligence hub, a report page or related insight.
2. Visitor selects a report category or custom report option.
3. Visitor reviews scope, sample output, methodology and limitations.
4. Visitor selects Commission a Report.
5. Visitor provides structured project information.
6. Tieko Media receives a secure notification and administrative record.
7. Visitor receives confirmation and next-step expectations.
8. The enquiry is qualified before pricing or engagement.

### 8.4 Insight-to-service journey

1. Visitor enters an article through search, referral or social media.
2. Visitor receives a complete and useful answer.
3. Contextual internal links connect the article to a service, report, product or case study.
4. Visitor may choose Book a Call or another appropriate next step.
5. The content interaction and conversion path are measurable.

### 8.5 Product proof journey

1. Visitor enters Products or a product detail page.
2. Visitor understands the problem, solution and intended users.
3. Visitor sees approved product media, status and evidence.
4. Visitor visits the live product where available.
5. Visitor can also explore Tieko’s product-development or digital-infrastructure capabilities.

### 8.6 Compliance journey

1. Visitor enters the Compliance service page.
2. The page explains Tieko Media’s coordination role.
3. The page identifies Vanteko & Associates as the provider of regulated legal services.
4. The page explains that an enquiry does not create a solicitor-client relationship.
5. The visitor provides only appropriate initial information.
6. Conflict-sensitive or confidential details are not requested prematurely.
7. The visitor books a call or submits an initial enquiry.
8. Information is routed according to the approved legal and privacy workflow.

## Appendix A: Digital Business Health Check product requirement

The Tieko Digital Business Health Check is a free interactive assessment and marketing tool that helps a business understand the strength of its digital foundation.

### Product outcome

The tool should help a user answer:

- How digitally sound is the business?
- Which foundations are strong?
- Which weaknesses are most urgent?
- What should be addressed first?
- When is a professional audit justified?

### Free assessment requirements

The MVP must:

- Take approximately three to five minutes
- Use plain-language questions
- Score eight defined dimensions
- Display the result immediately
- Provide an overall score from 0 to 100
- Show dimension-level results
- Identify three priority actions
- Recommend relevant services and insights
- Offer optional email delivery
- Offer a paid professional audit
- Include a clear indicative-result disclaimer
- Avoid collecting sensitive information

### Assessment dimensions

1. Business Identity and Compliance
2. Website and Technical Foundation
3. Search Discoverability
4. Content and Brand Consistency
5. Customer Acquisition and Conversion
6. Data, Analytics and Measurement
7. Reputation and Trust
8. Privacy and Security Readiness

### Paid audit conversion

The professional offer will be called the **Digital Presence and Infrastructure Audit** unless later renamed.

It should convert the self-assessment into an evidence-based engagement with verified findings, risks, priorities, quick wins and a sequenced roadmap.

### Originality requirement

Tieko may learn from assessment products used by other organisations, but the questions, scoring logic, copy, dimensions, result bands and recommendations must be original to Tieko Media.
