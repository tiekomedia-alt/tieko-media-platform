## 1. Purpose of this document

This document is the strategic source of truth for the ground-up rebuild of the Tieko Media website. It records the business vision, brand direction, public offering, website objectives, primary audiences, scope, constraints and confirmed decisions.

All subsequent documents, designs, Stitch prompts, engineering decisions and implementation work must remain consistent with this brief. Material changes must be recorded in the project decision and change log.

## 2. Project background

The previous Tieko Media website was created as an early single-page prototype. It does not adequately represent the present scope, ambition or capabilities of Tieko Media. Its architecture is not suitable for a multi-page service platform, product portfolio, research publication system, secure enquiry workflow or search-led growth strategy.

The new website will therefore be built from the ground up in a clean repository. The former website and repository will remain available only as a legacy content and asset reference.

## 3. Organisation overview

Tieko Media Limited is an African digital intelligence and infrastructure company based in Lagos, Nigeria. It helps businesses establish credible foundations, build useful digital systems, reach relevant audiences, understand their markets and optimise their digital presence.

Tieko Media is more than a conventional media agency. It combines:

- Digital strategy
- Media planning and buying
- Content and campaign systems
- Digital presence optimisation
- Research and commercial intelligence
- Digital product development
- Business automation
- Compliance coordination through qualified professional channels
- Public relations and reputation support
- Outdoor and traditional media execution

Tieko Media does not exist merely to generate temporary attention or viral content. It builds the infrastructure that helps organisations become visible, credible, compliant, measurable and prepared for sustainable growth.

## 14. Brand and visual direction

The website must feel African, intelligent, editorial, premium and contemporary.

### Established visual characteristics

- Warm off-white foundations
- Deep black or charcoal typography
- Tieko red as the primary expressive colour
- Restrained gold details
- Editorial grids
- Fine technical lines and registration marks
- Generous spacing
- Strong condensed display typography
- Editorial serif typography for selected statements
- Highly readable sans-serif typography for interfaces and body copy
- Authentic African people, environments and creative culture
- Tactile texture used carefully
- No neon styling
- No unnecessary glow
- No overcrowding
- No generic technology imagery where authentic context is available
- No em dashes in public-facing copy

### Tieko Àmì Ribbon System

The primary visual signature is a dynamic red system inspired by Yoruba àmì and African textile movement.

It has two connected expressions:

1. A sculptural, textured red ribbon that curls, folds, overlaps and enters compositions from different edges.
2. A flatter graphic àmì form that creates rhythm behind photography, diagrams and content.

The system may:

- Frame hero content
- Guide attention towards calls to action
- Create section transitions
- Crop or reveal imagery
- Appear behind selected cards
- Act as a restrained navigational or motion element
- Connect major sections visually

The Àmì Ribbon must never obstruct text, create accessibility problems, overwhelm mobile screens or introduce excessive performance cost. It should be simplified responsively and omitted where it does not improve the composition.

## 19. Security, privacy and data principles

Security must be designed into the system from the beginning.

The platform must include:

- Server-side validation
- Schema-based field validation
- Field allowlists
- Length and file-size restrictions
- Parameterised database operations
- Safe output encoding
- CSRF protection where applicable
- Rate limiting
- Bot and spam controls
- Secure file-upload handling if uploads are introduced
- MIME-type and file-signature validation
- Storage outside executable public directories
- Role-based access control
- Secure authentication for administrative functions
- Audit logging
- Secure headers and Content Security Policy
- Environment-variable protection
- Dependency monitoring
- Minimal personal-data collection
- Documented retention and deletion rules
- Consent and privacy records
- Incident-response procedures

Client-side validation must never be treated as the final security boundary.

The platform must comply with applicable Nigerian data-protection obligations and consider other relevant jurisdictions based on actual users and processing activities.

## 20. Accessibility principles

The platform should target WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Requirements include:

- Semantic HTML
- Keyboard accessibility
- Visible focus states
- Sufficient colour contrast
- Descriptive alternative text
- Accessible form labels and errors
- Reduced-motion support
- Correct heading hierarchy
- Accessible navigation and menus
- Screen-reader-compatible status messages
- Touch targets of appropriate size
- Captions or transcripts for relevant media

## 21. Performance principles

The engineering and design systems must establish budgets for:

- JavaScript
- Fonts
