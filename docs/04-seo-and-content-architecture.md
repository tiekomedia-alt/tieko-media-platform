## 1. Purpose

This document defines how Tieko Media will earn relevant search visibility, publish authoritative content and convert qualified visitors without producing generic, search-engine-first material.

It establishes:

- Search positioning and audience intent
- Page-level topic ownership
- Keyword and entity architecture
- Content pillars and editorial priorities
- Internal-linking rules
- On-page SEO requirements
- Local SEO and reputation rules
- Structured-data requirements
- AI search and answer-engine readiness
- Content production, review and maintenance
- Measurement and acceptance criteria

This document uses British English. Public website copy must not use em dashes.

## 2. SEO objective

Tieko Media should become a trusted search destination for African businesses and organisations that need stronger visibility, sound digital infrastructure, useful intelligence, credible communication and properly coordinated compliance support.

SEO is not a separate marketing layer. It must be built into the information architecture, page templates, content model, performance budget, analytics and publishing workflow from the beginning.

The programme has five commercial objectives:

1. Generate qualified Book a Call enquiries for the nine principal services as each enters its approved implementation phase.
2. Build authority around digital intelligence and infrastructure for African businesses.
3. Create demand for paid intelligence reports and commissioned research.
4. Demonstrate product-building capability through TeamShuffle and approved future products.
5. Use the Digital Business Health Check as an educational, search-led and campaign-led acquisition tool.

## 3. Research method and limitations

The initial topic map is based on:

- Live review of Nigerian and African service-market language
- Search-result language used by agencies, research providers and compliance firms
- Recognised terms used for digital audits and digital maturity assessments
- Current Google Search Central guidance
- Tieko Media's approved service architecture and commercial model
- User problems, rather than keyword repetition alone

This version does not invent monthly search volumes, keyword difficulty scores or traffic forecasts. Those figures require a confirmed target geography and live access to an approved keyword platform such as Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush or a comparable source.

Before final copy is approved, the keyword set should be validated for:

- Nigeria
- Lagos where locally relevant
- Selected African markets
- United Kingdom and United States only where Tieko has a credible offer and delivery pathway
- English-language variants and Nigerian phrasing
- Search intent, competition, seasonality and cost per click

Search volume must inform prioritisation, not dictate language. A lower-volume term with strong commercial intent may be more valuable than a broad high-volume term.

## 6. Keyword and topic principles

1. One primary search intent belongs to one canonical page.
2. Service pages target commercial intent. Articles answer narrower informational questions.
3. Location pages are only created when Tieko has real local relevance, differentiated information and a delivery pathway.
4. Synonyms should be used naturally. Exact-match repetition is prohibited.
5. Headings must describe the content, not merely contain keywords.
6. Every indexable page must provide original value.
7. Pages must not be created solely to capture minor keyword variations.
8. Claims, statistics and comparisons require evidence.
9. Search copy must sound like Tieko, not like a generic agency template.
10. The approved spelling is optimisation, organisation, programme and licence as a noun.

## 12. Content quality standard

Every substantive page must:

- Answer a defined audience need
- State who the content is for
- Demonstrate first-hand or professional understanding
- Use examples, processes, definitions or evidence
- Separate facts, interpretation and opinion
- Cite reliable sources where necessary
- Identify the author or responsible team
- Show the original publication date and meaningful update date
- Receive professional review where required
- Offer a relevant next step
- Avoid unsupported superlatives
- Avoid generic introductions and filler
- Avoid manufactured urgency
- Avoid fake testimonials, logos, statistics or case studies
- Avoid writing designed only to satisfy a word count

AI may assist research, outlining and editing, but no page should be published without human review, claim verification and Tieko-specific judgement.

## 15. Internal-linking architecture

### 15.1 Required relationships

- Each service page links to at least one complementary service, one useful insight and one relevant proof page when available.
- Each insight links to its primary commercial destination and two relevant educational pages.
- Each case study links to the service or services demonstrated.
- Each report links to the Intelligence hub, methodology and related insights.
- The Health Check links to relevant services based on each result dimension.
- The homepage links to priority hubs and selected commercial pages.
- Hub pages link to every approved child page.
- Breadcrumbs use crawlable HTML links.

### 15.2 Anchor text

Use descriptive natural language such as:

- Explore our digital presence optimisation service
- Learn how media planning differs from media buying
- Commission an African market intelligence report

Avoid repeated exact-match anchors, isolated click here labels and links hidden in scripts that search engines or assistive technology cannot use.

### 15.3 Orphan and depth controls

No indexable page may launch without:

- A link from an appropriate hub or established page
- XML sitemap inclusion
- A canonical URL
- A content owner
- A review date
- A meaningful route back into the user journey

Priority commercial pages should normally be reachable within three clicks from the homepage.

## 16. Local SEO and Google reputation

Tieko Media has stated that its Google business presence currently carries a five-star rating. This is an approved working fact supplied by the business owner, subject to live verification before publication.

Before displaying the rating, record:

- Exact Google Business Profile URL
- Business name shown on the profile
- Current rating
- Current review count
- Date and time verified
- Location or service-area configuration
- Permission to reproduce any individual review wording and reviewer identity

Recommended visible trust treatment:

- A restrained statement such as "Rated 5 stars on Google", only while accurate
- A direct link to the Google profile or reviews
- The review count beside the rating once verified
- A last-verified date in the CMS record
- No invented quotation or paraphrase attributed to a reviewer

Do not hard-code the claim without a maintenance owner. If the live rating changes, visible copy must be updated promptly.

### 16.1 Google Business Profile priorities

- Use the exact approved business name.
- Keep address or service area, telephone, website, hours and categories accurate.
- Link the profile to the canonical website.
- Publish original photos and approved updates.
- Request reviews without incentives or pressure.
- Respond professionally to positive and negative feedback.
- Use tagged website URLs where appropriate for measurement.
- Monitor duplicate or inaccurate listings.

## 17. Structured data

Use JSON-LD where appropriate. Markup must describe visible, accurate content and must not be used to manufacture search features.

| Page type | Recommended schema |
|---|---|
| Homepage | Organization or the most accurate business subtype, WebSite |
| Service page | Service, BreadcrumbList |
| Article | Article or BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, Person or Organization author |
| Report | Report where supported by Schema.org, CreativeWork, BreadcrumbList |
| Case study | Article or CreativeWork, BreadcrumbList |
| Product page | SoftwareApplication or Product only when properties are accurate |
| Team profile | Person |
| Contact or location | Organization or LocalBusiness details where accurate |
| FAQ content | FAQPage only if content is visible and current, without expecting a rich result |

### 17.1 Rating markup rule

Do not place self-serving AggregateRating or Review markup on Tieko's own organisation or local-business pages in an attempt to obtain review stars. Google limits review features for self-serving organisation and local-business reviews.

The verified five-star Google rating may be displayed visibly with a link to its source. It must not automatically become organisation-level review structured data.

- Logo
- Website URL
- Telephone number
- Email domain

## 18. AI search, AEO and GEO readiness

Tieko should optimise for human usefulness and machine comprehension without treating AI search as a separate trick.

Requirements:

- Provide a direct answer near the beginning of informational pages.
- Use explicit definitions and descriptive headings.
- Publish original methods, observations, frameworks and evidence.
- Make important information available as text, not only images or animation.
- Identify people, organisations, services and geographic scope consistently.
- Cite primary or authoritative sources.
- Show author, reviewer and update information.
- Keep pages crawlable and internally connected.
- Use structured data that matches visible content.
- Write concise summaries that can stand alone without stripping necessary nuance.
- Maintain accurate About, Team, Methodology and Contact pages.
- Monitor how Tieko is represented in major search and answer products.

There is no special Google AI schema required. Do not add unsupported markup or promise guaranteed inclusion in AI-generated answers.

## 19. Media and asset SEO

- Use descriptive filenames

- Use empty alt attributes when decorative
- Be served in efficient modern formats

## 20. Technical SEO requirements

Engineering must provide:

- Server-rendered or reliably rendered indexable content
- Correct 200, 301, 404 and 410 responses
- One canonical host and HTTPS
- Self-referencing canonical URLs on canonical pages
- XML sitemaps split by content type where scale requires it
- A clear robots.txt file
- Noindex for previews, internal search, test pages and campaign duplicates where appropriate
- 301 redirects for moved or retired URLs
- Fast, stable mobile pages
- Crawlable anchor links
- Correct hreflang only if true translated or regional alternatives exist
- Pagination that remains crawlable if archives become large
- Open Graph and social metadata
- Automated checks for missing titles, descriptions, H1s, canonicals and alt text
- Structured-data validation
- Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools configuration
- Privacy-aware analytics and conversion tracking

Campaign parameters must not create uncontrolled indexable duplicates.

## 21. Performance and page experience

SEO acceptance includes user experience.

Targets should be set in the TDD for:

- Largest Contentful Paint
- Interaction to Next Paint
- Cumulative Layout Shift
- Total JavaScript budget
- Image budget
- Font loading
- Third-party script control

The permanent navigation must not create layout shifts. The Àmì Ribbon and other brand motion must be progressively enhanced, respect reduced-motion preferences and never delay access to core content.
