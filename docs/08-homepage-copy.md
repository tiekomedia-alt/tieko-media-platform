## 14. Operating principles

### Eyebrow

**How we work**

### Heading

**Build the right thing before trying to scale it.**

### Principle 1

#### Strategy before noise

We define the problem, audience and intended outcome before choosing channels or deliverables.

### Principle 2

#### Systems before scale

Growth exposes weak foundations. We strengthen the underlying system before adding more traffic, content or complexity.

### Principle 3

#### Evidence before assumption

Research, measurement and real user behaviour should shape important decisions.

### Principle 4

#### Context before imitation

African businesses deserve work designed for their markets, constraints and ambitions, not copied templates with local names added.

### Principle 5

#### Clarity before complexity

A useful system should make the next decision easier. Technology and design must serve that purpose.

- Avoid generic office stock.
- Avoid over-polished artificial team scenes.
- Avoid AI artefacts.
- Obtain rights and releases.
- Provide meaningful alt text.
- Use empty alt text for decorative Àmì elements.

- Tieko Media team reviewing a campaign plan in Lagos
